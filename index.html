01 / 07 Who I am

About Me

I'm a Full Stack Web Developer based in Dhaka, working mostly on the systems companies run their operations on — ERP platforms, internal dashboards and the APIs behind them.

Most of my work starts with a business problem rather than a technical one: how payroll should actually be calculated, who is allowed to approve a transfer, why a report takes forty seconds to load. I spend as much time understanding the workflow as writing the Laravel code that implements it.

On the technical side my strength is the backend — secure role-based access, multi-tenant authentication, and keeping queries fast as tables grow into the millions with stored procedures and well-designed schemas.
