I'm a Full Stack Web Developer . Most of my work is the systems companies run their operations on — ERP and fleet-management platforms, internal dashboards, and increasingly the AI-assisted tools and Next.js frontends built on top of them.

That's meant building for Paragon, Sattar Metal, Sharif Metal, Parts Bazar, Elco and more — real businesses where the question is rarely technical first: how payroll should actually be calculated, who is allowed to approve a transfer, why a report takes forty seconds to load. I spend as much time understanding the workflow as writing the Laravel code that implements it.

On the backend my strength is secure role-based access, multi-tenant authentication, and keeping queries fast as tables grow into the millions with stored procedures and well-designed schemas — work that's lately extended into AI-driven matching and the Next.js frontends sitting on those same APIs.
